The DexVLA authors release official PyTorch code and ScaleDP stage weights but
do not publish a complete end-to-end robot checkpoint. This is the closest
public full checkpoint found. Its config2.json names the official
Qwen2VLForConditionalGenerationForVLA architecture and ScaleDP policy config;
the adapter maps its older DiT_H spelling to the official code's identical
ScaleDP_H constructor. No weights are converted.
