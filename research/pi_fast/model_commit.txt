Physical Intelligence does not publish a runnable PyTorch π0-FAST policy in
OpenPI. This experiment therefore uses LeRobot's maintained open-source
PyTorch implementation, a direct port designed to load LeRobot's public
π0-FAST checkpoints. No JAX model or checkpoint conversion is involved.
